Arcadia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Arcadia is an Android home screen launcher published by Arcadia Foundry. Contact: contact@arcadiafoundry.com.

The short version

Your phone. Your rules. Zero ads. No data sold. Built to respect your privacy.

Arcadia has no advertising and does not sell your personal data. You do not need an account. Your notifications and home-screen content are not used to build advertising profiles.

New versions of Arcadia can offer optional usage statistics and crash diagnostics through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These are separate, off-by-default choices in Arcadia Settings > Permissions. Collection starts only if you enable the relevant choice. Older versions without these controls do not include this optional collection. You can continue using Arcadia without enabling either choice.

Optional usage statistics

If you enable usage statistics, Google Analytics for Firebase processes app interactions and sessions, app version, device model, operating-system version, language, an app-instance identifier and approximate geographic information derived from the connection's IP address. This helps us understand active usage, engagement and which Arcadia versions people use. These are pseudonymous statistics, not a promise of anonymous data.

We do not send notification text or images, contacts, search terms, installed-app lists, names of apps you open, home-screen layouts, widget contents or the coordinates used by the clock feature as analytics events. Advertising-ID collection and advertising personalisation are disabled. We do not set a personal user ID or link analytics to advertising accounts.

Optional crash diagnostics

If you enable crash diagnostics, Firebase Crashlytics processes crash stack traces, exception information, diagnostic device and app information, timestamps and installation identifiers so we can investigate failures. Crash reports can contain technical details about what the app was doing. We do not add notification contents, search text, contacts or widget contents to diagnostic logs, and we do not assign a personal user ID.

Usage statistics and crash diagnostics are separate choices. You can enable one without the other. Turning either off stops future collection for that purpose; it does not erase reports already sent.

Data used on your phone

Installed apps and app usage. Arcadia reads installed apps to display, search and arrange them. If you grant Android usage access, Arcadia uses app-usage information locally for launcher features such as frequently used games. This Android permission is separate from optional Firebase usage statistics. The installed-app list and your usage of other apps are not sent as analytics events.

Notifications. Only if you grant notification access, Arcadia reads notifications to show badges, notification previews and related controls. If you enable notification history, it stores that history in private on-device storage. Arcadia does not upload notification history or include notification content in analytics events or custom diagnostic logs. Exception details in crash reports are described above. You can clear history in Arcadia and revoke access in Android settings. Notification history is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer by Arcadia's backup rules.

Accessibility API. Only if you enable the accessibility service, Arcadia uses Android's Accessibility API for double-tap to lock and the recent-apps action, including the dock gesture. The service does not read screen content or text you type, monitor other apps, or send accessibility content to Firebase. Arcadia is not an accessibility tool. Other features remain available if you do not enable the service.

Layout and preferences. Icon positions, folders, widgets and settings are stored in Arcadia's private app storage. Android's backup and device-transfer services may back up eligible settings according to your system configuration. Files you explicitly export, including manual or scheduled backups, are stored in the destination you choose; that destination may be a cloud provider. Uninstalling Arcadia does not delete exported files or copies held by backup providers.

Approximate location for the clock. If you grant approximate location permission, Arcadia uses city-level coordinates to calculate sunrise and sunset locally and caches them in private storage. Android's location and geocoding providers may process location requests, including coordinates to resolve a city name, under their own policies. Arcadia does not include these coordinates in Firebase analytics. Precise location is not requested. You can decline or revoke approximate location permission.

Widgets and actions from other apps. Widgets, app shortcuts, media providers and smart-home actions are supplied by the apps involved. Opening them or interacting with their services is governed by those apps' privacy policies. User-selected backup destinations and Android system services also have their own data handling.

Who processes optional data

Google provides Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Optional data is transmitted using encrypted connections and may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's applicable terms and international-transfer safeguards. Analytics is subject to Google's Analytics terms; Firebase services are subject to their applicable Firebase data-processing terms. We do not sell this data.

Read Google's privacy policy, Firebase privacy information and Google Analytics data practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app storage is removed when you clear Arcadia's storage or uninstall it, subject to Android backup/restore behaviour. Exported backups must be deleted at their chosen destination.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Google Analytics event-level retention is controlled by the property's retention setting; aggregated reports can have different retention. Contact us for the current setting and any data request. Uninstalling the app or withdrawing consent does not automatically delete previously transmitted analytics or diagnostics.

You may contact contact@arcadiafoundry.com to request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or ask about your rights under applicable law. We may need enough information to locate pseudonymous records, but do not send notification content or other unrelated sensitive information. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority, including the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

Your choices

  • Usage statistics and crash diagnostics: separately enable or disable them in Arcadia Settings > Permissions, when available in your version. Consent is not required to use the launcher.
  • Notification access, usage access, accessibility and approximate location: control them in Android settings.
  • Notification history: turn it off or clear it in Arcadia.
  • Local data: clear app storage or uninstall. Manage Android backups and exported files separately.

Optional analytics and diagnostics are processed on the basis of your consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already occurred before withdrawal.

Children

Arcadia is a general-purpose launcher and is not directed at children. Do not enable optional collection if you are below the applicable age for providing consent. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information through the optional services.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes. The current policy is available at https://arcadiafoundry.com/privacy-policy.html.
