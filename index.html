Features

Everything on the home screen, nothing in the way.

Long-press any app, get its world

Hold an icon and Arcadia opens a panel built around that app: its notifications, its shortcuts, and a live widget if you want one. Keep your finger down and scroll the list with a second finger — the panel stays put until you let go.

  • Notification history per app, with the ones you dismissed
  • Real app shortcuts, tappable while you hold
  • One widget per app, sized how you like

Your wallpaper, untouched

Arcadia never replaces or re-compresses your wallpaper. Android draws the real one behind the launcher and Arcadia just moves with it — including the preview in settings, which is a genuine window onto the live wallpaper rather than a copy.

  • Zoom and parallax handled by the system wallpaper engine
  • Live wallpapers work exactly as their author intended
  • No storage permission needed to show it

Privacy

It cannot send your data anywhere.
Not "does not" — cannot.

Arcadia does not declare Android's INTERNET permission. Without it the operating system refuses every outbound connection, whatever the app tries. That is enforced by the platform, not promised in a policy.

No INTERNET permission No analytics or crash reporting No ads, no ad SDK No accounts, no sign-in No precise location No all-files access

Notifications and approximate location are read on your device and stay in Arcadia's private storage. Uninstalling removes all of it.
