Hold any icon

Hold. Read. Let go.

Its live widget, its latest notifications and its shortcuts, right over your home screen. Tap what you need, let go, and you never left.

  • Its widget, live. Turn on the lights from the Home icon.
  • Its notifications. What it sent you, waiting under the icon.
  • Its shortcuts. Search Maps, or head home, in one tap.
  1. Hold. One finger on any icon. A buzz, and it lifts.
  2. Read. Its panel opens over Home. A second finger can tap a tile or a shortcut while the first holds.
  3. Let go. The panel folds back into the icon. Home is as you left it.
